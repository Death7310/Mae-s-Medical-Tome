# Cardiovascular Physiology
## Cardiac cycle and blood pressure

Sample lecture deck for Mae's Medical Tome

---

# Learning objectives

- Define cardiac output and its determinants
- Sequence the phases of the cardiac cycle
- Match the heart sounds to valve events
- Calculate mean arterial pressure
- Explain the baroreceptor reflex and why it is short-term

---

# Cardiac output

**CO = HR × SV**

- Rest: 70 bpm × 70 mL ≈ 5 L/min
- Exercise: up to 25 L/min
- SV = EDV − ESV
- Ejection fraction = SV / EDV ≈ 55–70 %

---

# What sets stroke volume?

| Determinant | Think of it as | Raised by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preload | End-diastolic stretch | Venous return |
| Afterload | Pressure to eject against | Aortic pressure |
| Contractility | Intrinsic force | Sympathetic drive |

Frank–Starling: more filling, more ejection

---

# Phases of the cardiac cycle

1. Atrial systole
2. Isovolumetric contraction (S1)
3. Ejection
4. Isovolumetric relaxation (S2)
5. Rapid filling
6. Diastasis

Systole ≈ 0.3 s, diastole ≈ 0.5 s at 75 bpm

---

# Heart sounds

- **S1** AV valves close, start of systole
- **S2** semilunar valves close, start of diastole
- **S3** rapid filling, normal in the young
- **S4** atrial kick into a stiff ventricle

Valves make noise closing, not opening

---

# Mean arterial pressure

**MAP ≈ DBP + ⅓ (SBP − DBP)**

- 120/80 → MAP ≈ 93 mmHg
- Pulse pressure = SBP − DBP ≈ 40 mmHg
- MAP ≈ CO × TPR
- Resistance ∝ 1 / r⁴: arterioles dominate

---

# Baroreceptor reflex

Carotid sinus (CN IX) + aortic arch (CN X) → NTS

- High BP → ↓ sympathetic, ↑ vagal → HR, SV, TPR fall
- Acts within 1–2 beats
- Resets in 1–2 days → not long-term control

---

# Long-term control

- RAAS: renin → Ang I → ACE → Ang II → vasoconstriction + aldosterone
- Kidney pressure natriuresis sets the long-term set point
- Hypertension thresholds: ACC/AHA ≥ 130/80, ESC/ESH ≥ 140/90
